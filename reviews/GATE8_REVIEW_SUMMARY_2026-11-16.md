# Gate 8 — Independent AI Scientific Review: Consolidated Findings

## Provenance limitation
The PI's session record identifies Gemini and Grok as two AI critics of the Experiment 2 results. Grok had also participated as a supplementary M4 behavioral rater. The original complete Gate 8 review transcripts and the authentication state of the AI sessions were **not** included in the materials available for this GitHub archive. This document records the review conclusions available from the project handoff; it does **not** purport to reconstruct verbatim reports, establish human peer review, or demonstrate that reviewer contexts were independent in every respect.

## Convergent scientific findings
1. **Primary P1 is formally inconclusive.** The preregistered minimum was ten operationally Unknown B/C pairs, but M3a yielded two. The observed −0.24-bit estimate with an interval spanning positive and negative differences cannot confirm, refute, or establish equivalence.
2. **Knownness measurement did not sufficiently predict free recall.** Although M3a classified 23 B items Known, only four of those 23 answers were correctly generated in the primary free-response format. Forced-choice preference should not be treated as a validated proxy for accessible factual recall, let alone a measure of internal knowledge.
3. **P2 is significant but unmatched.** Fictional C questions had substantially higher first-token entropy than familiar A Olympic questions, but familiar versus obscure topics and event domains differ. This does not isolate a model's detection of fictional event status.
4. **P3 does not show a statistically supported C-versus-B rejection preference shift.** The tested continuation templates measure conditional sequence scores, not the existence or absence of internally represented truth signals.
5. **The congress-series contrast is post hoc.** The strong association of H(C) − H(B) with the placement of the mathematics congress may explain substantial variance. It is not proof that congress series causally determines entropy or that truth status has no effect.
6. **M4 behavior is narrow evidence.** Near-universal production of city answers on fictional or impossible premises in this prompting setup cannot establish generalized model truth-indifference.
7. **Reproducibility and scientific validity are distinct.** Saved outputs and Gate 7 statistics were reproduced from the frozen analysis; source verification, reviewer provenance, design confounding, and narrow generalizability remain limitations.

## Review outcome
Both independent AI critiques accepted the work as a **feasibility study** with transparent negative and inconclusive findings, not as a confirmatory result for P1, the latent-space hypothesis, or collective-intelligence benefits. No additional model rerun was required by their documented conclusions.

## PI authorization and next step
The PI chose to report the work as a feasibility study and archive the methods, results, limitations, research-process failures, and V2 plans without changing frozen hypotheses or tests. The next experiment should validate knownness against free recall, control congress-series wording, independently verify event fictionality, and prospectively justify the analyzable sample size.
