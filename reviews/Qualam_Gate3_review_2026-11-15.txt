5. Verdict
GATE 3 CANNOT CLOSE on the two Modal runs alone. [HIGH] The remaining runs are necessary. As currently implemented, they can complete and write output even when a recorded identity or determinism check fails, and they do not by themselves provide the missing effective sampling and run-identity record.

Gate 3 blockers
- [HIGH] Final validation evidence. The retained decision-11 population, revised replacement pools, candidate identity and per-pair checks must agree in the final validation record. The supplied 19:16 JSON is an earlier failed run.
- [HIGH] Qwen engineering evidence. The outstanding Gate 3 outputs must establish the pinned checkpoint and tokenizer, valid-vocabulary dimensions, plausible logits/probabilities and repeat equality. The actual recorded values require inspection.
- [HIGH] Reproducible configuration and run identity. The bundled outputs and current metadata do not establish the complete effective generation configuration or unambiguously identify the selected stage, split and execution options. These fall directly under V0.4’s Gate 3 checklist.
Items to disclose and carry forward
- [HIGH] Before the pilot: M4’s fixed coding and second-rater procedure, the pilot familiar-answer and top-token checks, and the incomplete integrated M2 grouping. M4 coding need not be implemented as software to close Gate 3.
- [HIGH] Before relying on study analysis: the wrong P3 input to Holm, the fixed passing inputs for inconclusive conditions 3 and 5, and the missing behavioral summaries. These are substantive fidelity findings even though they are outside the eight-item Gate 3 checklist.
- [HIGH] In the engineering record: distinguish the logged HF/numpy comparison from a reproduced comparison, the mask rationale from a Qwen before/after check, and the toy cold run from evidence about Qwen. Nothing is frozen, and no pilot or main result is established.
