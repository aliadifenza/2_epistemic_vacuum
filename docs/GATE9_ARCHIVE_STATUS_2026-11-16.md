## Primary empirical finding

Only two of the thirty B/C pairs met M3a's operational Unknown criterion; the preregistered minimum was ten. P1 therefore remained inconclusive. Secondary P2 showed increased entropy for fictional C versus familiar A but cannot distinguish fictionality from topic familiarity. P3 did not show a statistically supported shift in rejection preference. Post-hoc congress-series effects are exploratory. These results cannot establish a modal geometry of latent space or model truth-indifference.
